Hi, I’m Efe.

Mehmet Efe Lorasdagi

I’m a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UCLA, advised by Prof. Vwani Roychowdhury.

I work on language models, reinforcement learning, and neural signal processing. I’m interested in how learning systems can improve with experience—from post-training language models to making sense of neural recordings.

Before UCLA, I completed my bachelor’s and master’s at Bilkent University, where I worked with Prof. Süleyman S. Kozat. I also spent two years building machine learning and retrieval systems at Databoss.

What I’m working on

Research interests

Language models & agents

LLM post-training, reinforcement learning, and information retrieval. Recent work includes controlled deep research through generative semantic workspaces.

Learning from neural signals

Deep learning for spike sorting, population event modeling, and multimodal models for understanding pathologic movements.

Learning over time

Continual learning, adaptive feature selection, and experience replay for continuous control.
